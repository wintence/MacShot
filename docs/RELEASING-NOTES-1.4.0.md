## What's new

- **Shadow color** — pick any color, or leave **Auto**: light on a dark background, dark on a light one.
- **Glow** — the window drawn again behind itself, blurred and semi-transparent, so the halo takes the colors of the shot. Blur and density adjustable, off by default.
- **Application name** — a small centered label above the window, taken from the `.exe` description: "Telegram Desktop", not "telegram.exe". On by default.
- The mouse wheel over a slider now scrolls the page instead of changing the value.
